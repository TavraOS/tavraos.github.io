Restaurant AI phone agent

More than an AI receptionist for restaurants

Tavra answers restaurant calls and moves the call into the right workflow: to-go orders, secure checkout, reservation details, custom answers, routing, notes, and staff notifications.

Try the AI Book a Demo

Why restaurants need a workflow-aware phone agent

A generic AI receptionist can answer a call. A restaurant phone agent needs to understand service pressure, guest requests, menu questions, reservation rules, staff routing, and when the call should become an operational record.

Orders

Take phone orders, answer menu questions, collect modifiers, read back the order, and support secure checkout when staff is busy.

Reservations

Collect party size, date, time, name, callback, allergies, notes, and special requests.

Routing

Transfer or escalate calls that need a manager, staff member, or human judgment.

Calls become handled restaurant work

  • To-go orders and menu questions can move through the restaurant's order and checkout workflow.
  • Reservation requests can use Tavra's Native Reservation Book or restaurant-configured rules.
  • Custom caller questions can be answered from configured business sources.
  • Staff-facing notes and notifications keep the restaurant informed without making staff catch every call live.
