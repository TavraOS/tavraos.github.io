Orders

To-go orders, handled from phone call to kitchen ticket

When a restaurant's POS and payment setup are configured, Tavra can take the caller's order, answer menu questions, confirm modifiers, collect payment, and send the order into the restaurant's normal prep flow.

If a caller asks for a manager or staff member in the middle of ordering, Tavra transfers immediately. Otherwise, the order path is built to be fully handled, accurately submitted, and ready for the kitchen.

Custom answers

Answers the questions your staff hears all day

Tavra can be configured with custom business knowledge for hours and open status, directions, parking, accessibility, wait times, walk-ins, allergies, large parties, private dining, catering, gift cards, loyalty, lost and found, complaints, hiring, live events, and entertainment.

Each category can be backed by the restaurant's preferred source: manual entries, website URLs, iCal feeds, Google Calendar, uploaded files, POS data, or other configured sources.

Events Tavra can sync with a restaurant's events page and answer questions about upcoming shows.
Details Cover charges, ticket links, age restrictions, start times, and related notes can be answered from the configured source.
Service Staff can stay focused while callers still get specific, useful answers.

Reservations

Reservations without another ringing phone

Tavra can collect the full reservation request by phone and use the restaurant's settings to decide what happens next. Confirm when rules allow it, store requests when staff approval is needed, or route the call when a human should take over.

The Native Tavra Reservation Book lets restaurants manage phone-originated reservations directly in Tavra while external provider integrations are added carefully over time.
