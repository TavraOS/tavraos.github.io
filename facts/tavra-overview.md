# Tavra Overview

Tavra is a restaurant AI phone operations platform. It helps restaurants answer calls and turn phone demand into handled workflows.

Tavra is built for restaurant phone operations, not generic chatbot use. Public Tavra messaging should emphasize restaurant workflows: to-go order taking, secure checkout, menu questions, reservations, custom caller questions, routing, notes, and staff or owner notifications.

Restaurants can configure Upsell rules for eligible same-item paid add-ons. The restaurant chooses the ordered item, paid modifier options, and optional wording; once required choices are resolved, Tavra makes a concise, line-specific offer for each eligible ordered item. Accepted add-ons are applied to the correct order line, while a decline closes that line and lets the conversation move on. This current capability is not a cross-sell, bundle, or AI-generated-offer system.

Tavra supports automatic English and Spanish call handling across to-go ordering, modifier choices, totals, checkout prompts, reservations, confirmations, and common restaurant questions. Configured menu item names remain intact. This should not be described as unrestricted translation of arbitrary owner-provided content.

The public website includes a live demo console that lets visitors configure a restaurant-style AI phone session and request a demo call.

Multi-location prospects may begin with one restaurant on regular Tavra Core at $399 per month before rolling out to the full restaurant group. This one-location evaluation is regular Tavra Core, not the Tavra Pilot Program. The evaluation window defaults to three months and may be shortened prospectively to two or one month.

Tavra should not be described with fake customer statistics, fake ratings, or unsupported integration claims.
