# Tavra Safety and Accuracy

Public Tavra pages must remain truthful and product-safe.

Do not publish:

- API keys, credentials, secrets, private endpoints, or private operational details.
- Fake customer numbers, fake revenue lift, fake saved hours, fake ratings, or fake reviews.
- Named customer deployments unless intentionally public and verified.
- Claims that Tavra replaces staff or replaces third-party restaurant systems.
- Claims that Tavra is approved by or affiliated with external providers unless verified and explicitly approved.

Torchy's may appear only as a demo or test-style scenario if already public in the repo. It must not be presented as a signed customer unless separately verified and approved.
