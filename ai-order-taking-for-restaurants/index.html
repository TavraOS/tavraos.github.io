AI order taking

To-go phone demand should not vanish during service

Tavra treats order calls as revenue workflows. It can answer menu questions, take the order, collect modifiers and caller details, read the order back, and move the caller through the restaurant's secure checkout path.

Try the AI Book a Demo

Order workflow capabilities

  • Take to-go orders over the phone.
  • Answer menu questions from the restaurant's configured menu and business information.
  • Collect item choices, modifiers, names, callback details, and notes depending on setup.
  • Read back the order and keep the caller moving toward secure checkout.
Current status

This page describes Tavra order-taking and secure checkout capabilities. Provider-specific POS submission, marketplace status, and third-party approvals are only described as active when they are live for that restaurant.

Menu questions

Callers can ask what is on the menu or what to order before they know exactly what they want.

Modifiers

Tavra can collect item details, handle corrections, and read back the order before checkout.

Handoff

If a caller asks for a manager or staff member, Tavra can route the call according to restaurant settings.
