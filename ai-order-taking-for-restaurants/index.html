AI order taking

To-go phone demand should not vanish during service

Tavra treats order calls as revenue workflows. It can answer menu questions, capture order intent, collect the details the restaurant needs, and move callers through the configured order path.

Try the AI Book a Demo

Order workflow capabilities

  • Understand when a caller wants food to go.
  • Answer menu questions from the restaurant's configured menu and business information.
  • Collect item choices, modifiers, names, callback details, and notes depending on setup.
  • Read back the order and keep the caller moving toward the restaurant's configured next step.
Current status

This page describes Tavra order workflow capabilities and configuration concepts. It does not claim live POS injection, live card charges, marketplace listing, or provider approval unless those claims are separately verified and approved.

Menu questions

Callers can ask what is on the menu or what to order before they know exactly what they want.

Modifiers

Tavra can collect the details needed to make an order understandable to staff or the configured order workflow.

Handoff

If a caller asks for a manager or staff member, Tavra can route the call according to restaurant settings.
