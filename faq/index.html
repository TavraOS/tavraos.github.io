Tavra restaurant AI phone FAQ

Concise answers about Tavra's restaurant phone workflows, reservations, order intent, integrations, payments, pilots, and demo boundaries.

What is Tavra?
Tavra is a restaurant AI phone operations platform that answers calls and helps move callers into workflows such as orders, reservations, custom answers, routing, notes, and staff notifications.
Is Tavra a generic chatbot?
No. Tavra is designed around restaurant phone workflows, not generic chatbot conversations.
Can Tavra take to-go orders?
Tavra can handle order intent, menu questions, and order details depending on restaurant configuration. Public pages should not imply unsupported live POS injection or payment processing.
Can Tavra handle reservations?
Yes. Tavra can capture reservation details and supports Native Tavra Reservation Book workflows with restaurant-configurable rules.
Does Tavra replace staff?
No. Tavra helps handle phone workflows and route calls when a human should step in.
Can Tavra transfer calls to a human?
Yes. Tavra can route calls to configured handoff paths when a caller needs staff or manager help.
Does Tavra integrate with POS systems?
Tavra is built around restaurant workflow and provider-safe integration concepts. Named provider availability should be described only when verified and approved.
Does Tavra process payments?
Payment-related workflows depend on restaurant setup and approved provider configuration. The public demo should not be treated as a request to enter card details.
Is Tavra available for pilots?
Tavra is preparing restaurant pilots. Operators can request a demo through the public website.
How does the demo work?
The web demo lets visitors configure a sample restaurant phone agent and request a demo call. It is a public demo and should not be interpreted as proof of live third-party provider approval.
