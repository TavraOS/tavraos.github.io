Tavra restaurant AI phone FAQ

Concise answers about Tavra's restaurant phone workflows, reservations, order intent, integrations, payments, pilots, demo boundaries, and what to ask during evaluation.

What is Tavra?
Tavra is a restaurant AI phone operations platform that answers calls and helps move callers into workflows such as orders, reservations, custom answers, routing, notes, and staff notifications.
Is Tavra a generic chatbot?
No. Tavra is designed around restaurant phone workflows, not generic chatbot conversations.
Can Tavra take to-go orders?
Tavra can handle order intent, menu questions, and order details depending on restaurant configuration. Public pages should not imply unsupported live POS injection or payment processing.
Can Tavra handle reservations?
Yes. Tavra can capture reservation details and supports Native Tavra Reservation Book workflows with restaurant-configurable rules.
Does Tavra replace staff?
No. Tavra helps handle phone workflows and route calls when a human should step in.
Can Tavra transfer calls to a human?
Yes. Tavra can route calls to configured handoff paths when a caller needs staff or manager help.
Does Tavra integrate with POS systems?
Tavra is built around restaurant workflow and provider-safe integration concepts. Named provider availability should be described only when verified and approved.
Does Tavra process payments?
Payment-related workflows depend on restaurant setup and approved provider configuration. The public demo should not be treated as a request to enter card details.
Is Tavra available for pilots?
Tavra is preparing restaurant pilots. Operators can request a demo through the public website.
How does the demo work?
The web demo lets visitors configure a sample restaurant phone agent and request a demo call. It is a public demo and should not be interpreted as proof of live third-party provider approval.

Questions to ask during any restaurant AI phone demo

Use these questions when evaluating Tavra or any restaurant AI phone system. The important thing is to separate active capabilities from planned integrations, sales shorthand, and provider-dependent setup.

What should restaurants ask about POS and reservation integrations?
Ask whether each connection is active, direct, approved, and specific to your provider and account. Tavra public pages distinguish current Tavra workflow capabilities from planned or approval-dependent provider paths. Do not assume a named provider is live unless that status is stated clearly.
What happens if internet service or a connected system is unavailable during a call?
Failure behavior should be configured and verified during setup. Tavra should not promise live POS or reservation-system fallback behavior unless that behavior is active for the restaurant. A responsible setup should avoid confirming actions that depend on unavailable systems.
Can Tavra handle live inventory changes?
Tavra should only answer from live inventory when the restaurant has a reliable, current inventory source connected for that workflow. If live inventory is not configured, Tavra should not claim real-time stock awareness.
How does Tavra handle complex modifiers or caller changes during an order?
Tavra is designed for restaurant order conversations, including item details and readbacks, but each restaurant should test its actual menu, modifiers, and common correction patterns during demo or pilot before relying on them operationally.
What causes Tavra to transfer a call, and what context does staff receive?
Tavra supports configured handoff routes and staff-facing call records. Exact transfer triggers, routing destinations, and the context staff receives should be configured and verified for each restaurant.
How does Tavra handle accents or noisy calls?
No voice system is perfect. Tavra should confirm important details, ask clarifying questions, and route or collect for review when confidence or context is not sufficient. Restaurants should test realistic call conditions during evaluation.
How should restaurants evaluate Tavra pricing?
Confirm what is included in the plan and whether any usage, transaction, setup, payment, support, or call-volume costs apply. Tavra public pages should not imply final pricing terms beyond the published pilot options and approved agreements.
Can managers update menus, custom answers, and FAQs themselves?
Tavra is built around restaurant-controlled configuration concepts, including custom caller-answer categories. Buyers should confirm which edits managers can make directly, which require Tavra support, and whether any update fees apply.
